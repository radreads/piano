## 6. Practice Philosophy (Mental Models)

Any LLM coaching me on piano/music should align with:

### 6.1 Three Operating Systems

Every session should touch 2–3 of:

1. **Physical OS** – technique, posture, relaxation, finger control
2. **Rhythmic OS** – internal pulse, subdivisions, groove
3. **Harmonic OS** – chords, scales, functional harmony

### 6.2 Shapes First, Names Second

- Treat chords as **hand shapes** and **grips** anchored to physical patterns.
- Labels (C/E, Am7, etc.) are important but can follow the embodied pattern.

### 6.3 Numbers / Functions > Letters

- Use Roman numerals / Nashville system:
  - I, IV, V, vi, etc.
- This enables:
  - transposition,
  - pattern recognition,
  - fast mapping between keys and styles.

### 6.4 Tiny Loops, Not Whole Songs (Initially)

- Prefer practicing **2–4 bar chunks**:
  - of pieces (e.g., Einaudi)
  - of progressions
  - of rhythmic motifs
- Loop-based thinking maps to:
  - electronic music structure
  - efficient skill acquisition

### 6.5 Slow + Correct

- Always start **slower than comfortable**.
- If same mistake appears twice, reduce:
  - tempo or
  - chunk size.
- Prioritize **clean reps** to avoid relearning.

### 6.6 Constrained Sandbox

Early months:
- Focus keys: **C major, A minor** (then add G, D, etc.).
- Focus chords: C, F, G, Am, Em + diatonic triads.
- One or two main repertoire pieces.
- Limited rhythmic patterns → depth over variety.

### 6.7 Interleaved Practice

Within each 30-minute session:
- Mix:
  - brief technique
  - brief theory/harmony
  - brief repertoire
  - occasional free improv or DAW application

No long single-mode binges (e.g., 40 minutes of passive theory videos).

### 6.8 Self-Recording Feedback

- Regularly record 30–60 sec clips to:
  - evaluate timing,
  - evenness,
  - tension,
  - musicality.
- Make **one specific adjustment** per review.

### 6.9 Producer Mode Mapping

Whenever a new pattern is learned, ask:
- “How can this become a loop, arp, pad, or topline in my tracks?”

Piano is:
- not separate from production,
- but the **idea generator** and **R&D lab**.

### 6.10 Metrics: Leading Indicators

Focus on:
- Sessions per week
- Smoothness of key drills
- Ease of specific progressions
- Comfort with small passages of chosen piece

Not on:
- Comparing to pros
- Needing many full songs
