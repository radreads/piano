# Month 1: Weekly Exercises & Practice Plan
**Theme:** Foundation & Physical Setup, Bass Clef Basics, C Major Sandbox

**Total Practice Time:** ~4-5 hours/week (30 min/day weekdays + 1-2 longer weekend sessions)

---

## Week 1: Posture, Hand Shape & Right Hand Foundation

### Goals
- Establish correct physical setup
- Learn C major 5-finger pattern (RH)
- Begin bass clef landmark recognition
- Start metronome comfort

### Daily Practice Routine (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Physical Setup Check**
- Bench height: Elbows at ~90° angle when hands on keys
- Seat distance: Sit at front third of bench, comfortable reach
- Posture: Straight back, relaxed shoulders, feet flat on floor
- Hand shape: Curved fingers (hold tennis ball shape), relaxed wrist

**Minutes 5-12: Right Hand 5-Finger Pattern Drills**
- **Exercise 1A:** C-D-E-F-G ascending (fingers 1-2-3-4-5)
  - Play each note slowly, listening for even tone
  - Tempo: 60 BPM, quarter notes
  - Reps: 10x ascending

- **Exercise 1B:** G-F-E-D-C descending (fingers 5-4-3-2-1)
  - Same tempo and focus
  - Reps: 10x descending

- **Exercise 1C:** Combined pattern
  - Up: C-D-E-F-G
  - Down: G-F-E-D-C
  - Reps: 5x complete cycles

**Minutes 12-20: C Major Scale Introduction (RH)**
- **Exercise 2A:** Thumb-under practice (slow motion)
  - Play C-D-E (1-2-3)
  - Tuck thumb under to play F (1)
  - Continue E-F-G (4-5)
  - Practice thumb movement separately, very slowly
  - Reps: 5x just the thumb crossing

- **Exercise 2B:** Full C major scale (RH)
  - Fingering: 1-2-3-1-2-3-4-5 ascending
  - Fingering: 5-4-3-2-1-3-2-1 descending
  - Tempo: 50 BPM, quarter notes
  - Focus: Smooth thumb crossing, no hesitation
  - Reps: 5x up and down

**Minutes 20-27: Bass Clef Landmark Notes**
- **Exercise 3A:** Landmark identification (flashcard style)
  - Draw bass clef staff on paper or use app
  - Mark these notes: F (below middle C), A, C (middle C), E, G
  - Quiz yourself: Point to a line/space, name the note
  - Reps: 2 minutes identifying notes

- **Exercise 3B:** Play landmark notes (LH)
  - Find and play each landmark: F-A-C-E-G
  - Say note name aloud as you play
  - Use any fingers for now (focus on finding notes)
  - Reps: 5x through all landmarks

**Minutes 27-30: Free Exploration**
- Improvise using only white keys
- Try different combinations of C-D-E-F-G in RH
- Experiment with soft vs loud playing
- No rules, just explore the instrument

### Specific Exercises for Week 1

#### Exercise W1-1: "Finger Independence" (Daily, 5 min)
```
RH on C-D-E-F-G position
Play each finger 4x while others stay on keys:
1-1-1-1 (C four times)
2-2-2-2 (D four times)
3-3-3-3 (E four times)
4-4-4-4 (F four times)
5-5-5-5 (G four times)

Keep unused fingers lightly touching keys, no lifting!
```

#### Exercise W1-2: "Even Tone Test" (3x per week, 3 min)
```
RH C major 5-finger pattern
Play C-D-E-F-G very slowly (one note per 2 seconds)
Goal: Every note EXACTLY same volume
Record yourself, listen back
Identify which fingers are louder/softer
Adjust and repeat
```

#### Exercise W1-3: "Bass Clef Scavenger Hunt" (Daily, 3 min)
```
Without looking at piano:
1. Draw bass clef on paper
2. Mark F (4th line), A (2nd space), C (2nd line), E (3rd space), G (top line)
3. Then find these notes on piano with LH
4. Play them in order: F-A-C-E-G
5. Then backwards: G-E-C-A-F
```

#### Exercise W1-4: "Metronome Friendship" (Daily, 3 min)
```
Set metronome to 60 BPM
Just listen for 30 seconds
Tap foot on each beat for 30 seconds
Clap hands on each beat for 30 seconds
Play C note (RH) on each beat for 1 minute
Goal: Feel the pulse in your body
```

### Weekend Long Session (60 min)
**Use Saturday or Sunday for deeper practice**

- 0-10 min: Full physical setup + warm-up
- 10-25 min: C major scale practice (slow, perfect reps)
- 25-40 min: All week's exercises in sequence
- 40-50 min: Bass clef note reading practice
- 50-60 min: Free play + experiment with dynamics (pp to ff)

### Week 1 Checkpoints
By end of Week 1, you should:
- [ ] Sit at piano with correct posture automatically
- [ ] Play C-D-E-F-G (RH) smoothly at 60 BPM
- [ ] Execute thumb-under for C major scale (even if slow)
- [ ] Identify all 5 bass clef landmarks without hesitation
- [ ] Feel comfortable practicing with metronome at 60 BPM

## Week 2: Left Hand Engagement & Root Position Triads

### Goals
- Begin LH 5-finger patterns
- Learn C, F, G, Am triads (RH root position)
- Continue bass clef fluency
- Introduce quarter + half note rhythms

### Daily Practice Routine (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Physical Warm-Up**
- Posture check
- Shoulder rolls (5 forward, 5 backward)
- Wrist circles (5 each direction)
- RH C major scale 2x (from Week 1)

**Minutes 5-12: Left Hand 5-Finger Pattern**
- **Exercise 2A:** LH C position (C-B-A-G-F)
  - Fingers: 5-4-3-2-1 descending
  - Mirror of RH pattern
  - Tempo: 60 BPM
  - Reps: 10x descending

- **Exercise 2B:** LH ascending (F-G-A-B-C)
  - Fingers: 1-2-3-4-5
  - Reps: 10x ascending

- **Exercise 2C:** LH full pattern
  - Up: F-G-A-B-C
  - Down: C-B-A-G-F
  - Reps: 5x complete

**Minutes 12-22: Root Position Triads (RH)**
- **Exercise 2D:** Triad shapes (1-3-5 fingering)

  **C major triad:** C-E-G (fingers 1-3-5)
  - Practice shape: place all 3 fingers simultaneously
  - Lift, place again, listen to sound
  - Reps: 10x

  **F major triad:** F-A-C (fingers 1-3-5)
  - Same process
  - Reps: 10x

  **G major triad:** G-B-D (fingers 1-3-5)
  - Same process
  - Reps: 10x

  **A minor triad:** A-C-E (fingers 1-3-5)
  - Same process
  - Reps: 10x

- **Exercise 2E:** Triad sequence drill
  - Play: C - F - G - C (each triad as block chord)
  - Tempo: 60 BPM, 1 chord per 2 beats
  - Reps: 5x through progression

**Minutes 22-27: Bass Clef Reading Advancement**
- **Exercise 2F:** Extended range
  - Learn: G (below F), B, D, F (top line)
  - Now know full F2-G3 range (10 notes)
  - Play ascending: G-A-B-C-D-E-F-G
  - LH fingering: 5-4-3-2-1-3-2-1

**Minutes 27-30: Rhythm Introduction**
- Quarter notes vs half notes
- Clap rhythm: ♩ ♩ 𝅗𝅥 (quarter-quarter-half)
- Play on C: tap rhythm pattern 5x

### Specific Exercises for Week 2

#### Exercise W2-1: "Triad Spelling Quiz" (Daily, 5 min)
```
Say aloud (then play):
"C major triad is: C - E - G" [play it]
"F major triad is: F - A - C" [play it]
"G major triad is: G - B - D" [play it]
"A minor triad is: A - C - E" [play it]

Repeat until you can do all 4 without thinking
Goal: <3 seconds per triad (name + play)
```

#### Exercise W2-2: "LH-RH Mirror" (3x per week, 5 min)
```
Play 5-finger pattern hands separately:
- RH: C-D-E-F-G (ascending)
- LH: C-B-A-G-F (descending)

Then play both together (mirror motion):
RH goes up while LH goes down simultaneously
Start very slowly (50 BPM)
Focus: Both hands even tone
```

#### Exercise W2-3: "Shape Recognition" (Daily, 3 min)
```
Close your eyes
Feel the C major triad shape (C-E-G)
Lift hands, place on C triad again without looking
Repeat for F, G, Am triads
Goal: Hand "knows" the shape, not just the notes
```

#### Exercise W2-4: "Rhythm Clap & Play" (Daily, 3 min)
```
Pattern: ♩ ♩ 𝅗𝅥 ♩ ♩ 𝅗𝅥 (quarter-quarter-half, repeat)

Step 1: Clap the rhythm 5x
Step 2: Play rhythm on C (RH) 5x
Step 3: Play rhythm on C triad (block chord) 5x
Step 4: Play rhythm on progression: C-F-G-C
```

### Weekend Long Session (60 min)

- 0-10 min: Warm-up (both hands scales/patterns)
- 10-30 min: Triad mastery (all 4 triads, perfect placement)
- 30-45 min: LH bass clef reading (play simple bass lines)
- 45-55 min: Rhythm exercises with triads
- 55-60 min: Improvise using only C, F, G, Am triads

### Week 2 Checkpoints
By end of Week 2, you should:
- [ ] Play LH 5-finger pattern as smoothly as RH
- [ ] Name and play all 4 triads (C, F, G, Am) in <5 seconds each
- [ ] Read bass clef notes G2-G3 (full octave + one note)
- [ ] Execute simple rhythms (quarters and halves) accurately
- [ ] Feel shape of each triad in your hand

## Week 3: Hand Coordination & Einaudi Piece Selection

### Goals
- Begin hands-together playing
- Select and analyze Einaudi repertoire piece
- Introduce static coordination (RH triad + LH root)
- Expand bass clef to include ledger lines

### Daily Practice Routine (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Warm-Up**
- RH C major scale 2x (from Week 1)
- LH 5-finger pattern 2x (from Week 2)
- All 4 triads 1x each (from Week 2)

**Minutes 5-15: Static Hand Coordination**
- **Exercise 3A:** RH triad + LH single note (whole notes)

  **Position 1:** RH plays C triad, LH plays C (low)
  - Place both hands simultaneously
  - Hold for 4 beats
  - Release together
  - Reps: 5x

  **Position 2:** RH plays F triad, LH plays F
  - Same process
  - Reps: 5x

  **Position 3:** RH plays G triad, LH plays G
  - Reps: 5x

  **Position 4:** RH plays Am triad, LH plays A
  - Reps: 5x

- **Exercise 3B:** Simple progression hands-together
  - Progression: C (both hands) - F (both hands) - G (both hands) - C (both hands)
  - Each chord held for 4 beats
  - Tempo: 60 BPM
  - Reps: 5x through progression

**Minutes 15-22: Einaudi Piece Work**
- **Exercise 3C:** Piece selection
  - Choose one: "Nuvole Bianche," "Una Mattina," "Le Onde," or "I Giorni"
  - Find sheet music or tutorial
  - Listen to recording 2-3x

- **Exercise 3D:** First 4 bars analysis
  - Identify the key
  - Identify chord progression (Roman numerals if possible)
  - Identify RH melodic shape (ascending/descending/static)

- **Exercise 3E:** RH melody only (bars 1-4)
  - Learn first 4 bars, RH only
  - Tempo: 40 BPM (very slow)
  - Focus: Correct notes, correct fingering
  - Reps: Play through 5x slowly

**Minutes 22-27: Moving Bass Line Introduction**
- **Exercise 3F:** LH quarter note roots
  - Play: C-C-C-C (4 quarter notes on low C)
  - Then: F-F-F-F
  - Then: G-G-G-G
  - Then: C-C-C-C
  - All with metronome at 60 BPM

- **Exercise 3G:** RH simple melody + LH roots
  - RH plays: C-D-E-C (over 4 beats)
  - LH plays: C (quarter notes, 4x)
  - Repeat this 2-bar pattern 5x
  - Then try with different chord: F, G

**Minutes 27-30: Free Improvisation**
- RH improvise simple melody using C-D-E-F-G
- LH drone on C (just hold the note)
- Experiment with different rhythms
- Record 30 seconds if possible

### Specific Exercises for Week 3

#### Exercise W3-1: "Synchronization Drill" (Daily, 5 min)
```
Both hands play the SAME note simultaneously:
- Both hands on middle C together (press and release together 10x)
- Both hands on D together (10x)
- Both hands on E together (10x)

Goal: Perfectly synchronized attack and release
Listen: Should sound like ONE piano, not two
```

#### Exercise W3-2: "Shell Voicing Builder" (Daily, 5 min)
```
RH plays triad, LH plays root:

C position: RH = C-E-G, LH = C (octave lower)
Hold 4 beats, release together

F position: RH = F-A-C, LH = F
Hold 4 beats

G position: RH = G-B-D, LH = G
Hold 4 beats

Progression practice: C-F-G-C (each chord 4 beats)
Reps: 3x through, hands together, smooth transitions
```

#### Exercise W3-3: "Einaudi Shape Study" (3x per week, 5 min)
```
From your chosen piece, bars 1-4:

1. Sing/hum the melody
2. Play RH melody ONLY (slow)
3. Identify the shape: does it go up? down? repeat?
4. Mark fingering on sheet music
5. Play RH 5x perfectly at 40 BPM

Don't add LH yet! Just RH mastery.
```

#### Exercise W3-4: "Independent Rhythm" (3x per week, 5 min)
```
Pattern: RH plays whole note, LH plays quarter notes

Bar 1:
- RH: C (whole note, hold 4 beats)
- LH: C-C-C-C (quarter notes)

Bar 2:
- RH: E (whole note)
- LH: C-C-C-C (quarter notes)

Continue with different RH notes over same LH pattern
Goal: Hands have different rhythms, stay independent
```

### Weekend Long Session (60 min)

- 0-10 min: Full warm-up (scales, triads, coordination)
- 10-30 min: Einaudi piece deep dive (bars 1-4, RH perfection)
- 30-45 min: Hands-together coordination drills
- 45-55 min: Bass clef reading advancement
- 55-60 min: Play progression C-F-G-C hands-together, record it

### Week 3 Checkpoints
By end of Week 3, you should:
- [ ] Play C-F-G-C progression hands-together (static chords)
- [ ] Have selected Einaudi piece and learned bars 1-4 (RH only)
- [ ] Execute synchronized attacks (both hands together precisely)
- [ ] Play simple independent rhythms (RH whole, LH quarters)
- [ ] Understand harmonic structure of chosen piece (first 4 bars)

## Week 4: Integration Week & First Hands-Together Piece

### Goals
- Integrate all Week 1-3 skills
- Play first simple 8-bar piece hands-together
- Solidify all 4 triads in all positions
- Record first progress video

### Daily Practice Routine (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Integrated Warm-Up**
- C major scale RH (2 octaves if possible, otherwise 1 octave)
- C major scale LH (1 octave)
- All 4 triads RH: C-F-G-Am sequence
- Bass clef landmarks: Play F-A-C-E-G (LH)

**Minutes 5-15: Einaudi Piece Development**
- **Exercise 4A:** Bars 1-4 hands separate polish
  - RH melody: Play 3x at 50 BPM
  - LH bass/chords: Learn and play 3x at 50 BPM
  - Identify how hands fit together

- **Exercise 4B:** Bars 1-2 hands together
  - Combine hands for just first 2 bars
  - Tempo: 40 BPM (very slow)
  - Play correct notes, even if tempo is extremely slow
  - Reps: 10x without stopping

- **Exercise 4C:** Bars 3-4 hands together
  - Same process as bars 1-2
  - Tempo: 40 BPM
  - Reps: 10x

- **Exercise 4D:** Bars 1-4 complete
  - Full 4 bars hands together
  - Tempo: 40 BPM
  - Goal: Play through without stopping (even with mistakes)
  - Reps: 5x

**Minutes 15-22: Triad Inversions Introduction (Theory)**
- **Exercise 4E:** Understanding inversions
  - C major root position: C-E-G (1-3-5)
  - C major 1st inversion: E-G-C (1-2-5)
  - C major 2nd inversion: G-C-E (1-2-5)
  - Just learn the concept, play each slowly
  - Don't need to master yet, just exposure

**Minutes 22-27: Simple 8-Bar Piece Hands-Together**
- **Exercise 4F:** "My First Progression" (original exercise)

```
8-bar piece in C major, hands together:

Bars 1-2:
RH: C-D-E-D-C-D-E-D (quarter notes)
LH: C (whole note) | C (whole note)

Bars 3-4:
RH: E-F-G-F-E-F-G-F
LH: F (whole note) | F (whole note)

Bars 5-6:
RH: G-A-G-F-E-D-C-D
LH: G (whole note) | G (whole note)

Bars 7-8:
RH: E-D-C (half note + quarter note + whole note)
LH: C (whole note) | C (whole note)
```

- Learn hands separate first (2 minutes each hand)
- Then combine at 50 BPM
- Reps: 3x through complete piece

**Minutes 27-30: Progress Documentation**
- Record yourself playing:
  - C major scale (RH)
  - C-F-G-Am triads
  - Einaudi bars 1-4 (even if slow/imperfect)
- Listen back, note one thing to improve

### Specific Exercises for Week 4

#### Exercise W4-1: "Triad Fluency Speed Test" (Daily, 5 min)
```
Set timer for 60 seconds:
How many times can you play C-F-G-Am-G-F-C progression?

Week 4 goal: At least 5 complete cycles in 60 seconds
Each triad = block chord, RH only
Focus: Accuracy over speed, but build speed
```

#### Exercise W4-2: "Two-Bar Loop Mastery" (Daily, 5 min)
```
Choose any 2 bars from Einaudi piece
Play those 2 bars on repeat, hands together
Tempo: Start 40 BPM, increase by 5 BPM each session
Goal: 10 perfect repetitions at current tempo
If you make 2 mistakes, reduce tempo 10 BPM
```

#### Exercise W4-3: "Bass + Melody Independence" (3x per week, 5 min)
```
LH plays: C-C-C-C (quarter notes) on repeat (4-bar loop)
RH improvises melody using C-D-E-F-G over the top

Then:
LH plays: C-F-G-C progression (whole notes)
RH improvises different melody over progression

Goal: LH steady and automatic, RH free to explore
```

#### Exercise W4-4: "Listening Analysis" (2x per week, 10 min)
```
Listen to your chosen Einaudi piece:
1. Identify when chords change (mark timestamps)
2. Try to play along with just the bass notes (LH)
3. Hum the melody
4. Notice dynamics (pp, mp, mf, f, ff)
5. Notice tempo changes (if any)

Write down observations for practice guidance
```

### Weekend Long Session (60 min)

- 0-10 min: Complete warm-up routine
- 10-30 min: Einaudi piece (bars 1-4 refinement, add bars 5-8 RH only)
- 30-45 min: 8-bar simple piece mastery (smooth, musical)
- 45-55 min: Triad review + inversion exploration
- 55-60 min: Free improvisation using week's skills

### Month 1 Final Checkpoints
By end of Week 4 (Month 1 complete), you should:
- [ ] C major scale fluent (RH 2 oct, LH 1 oct) at 80 BPM
- [ ] All 4 triads (C, F, G, Am) instant recall and clean execution
- [ ] Bass clef reading: Identify F2-G3 range without hesitation
- [ ] Play 4-8 bars of Einaudi piece hands-together (even if slow)
- [ ] Execute simple hands-together coordination (static chords, simple melodies)
- [ ] Understand metronome pulse and can play in time
- [ ] Completed at least 20 practice sessions in Month 1
- [ ] Recorded at least one progress video

## Producer Integration Ideas for Month 1

### Week 1-2: Capture Foundation
- Record C major scale at different velocities → create velocity layers for sampling
- Record single notes pp, mp, mf, f, ff → build custom piano sample pack

### Week 3: Chord Progressions
- Record C-F-G-C progression as MIDI → import to DAW
- Try different tempos: 90 BPM (house), 120 BPM (techno), 140 BPM (breaks)
- Layer with synth playing same progression

### Week 4: First Loop
- Record your 8-bar simple piece as MIDI
- Process through reverb, delay for atmospheric sound
- Use as foundation for minimal progressive house track
- Add drum loop under your piano playing

## Month 1 Reflection Questions

At the end of Month 1, ask yourself:

1. **Physical:** Is my posture automatic? Any tension in shoulders/wrists?
2. **Technical:** Can I play C major scale smoothly without thinking about fingering?
3. **Harmonic:** Do I understand what makes C, F, G major and Am minor?
4. **Rhythmic:** Am I comfortable with metronome? Can I feel the pulse internally?
5. **Repertoire:** How much of my Einaudi piece can I play hands-together?
6. **Reading:** Can I read bass clef landmarks instantly?
7. **Consistency:** Did I hit my 5 sessions/week target most weeks?
8. **Production:** Have I captured any piano ideas into my DAW?
9. **Enjoyment:** Am I having fun? What feels best about practice?
10. **Next:** What from Month 2 am I most excited about?

**Write answers and keep for future reference. Compare progress in Month 6 and Month 12.**
