# 12-Month Piano Syllabus for Khe
*Designed for ~4-5 hours/week practice, aligned with electronic production goals*

---

## Overview & Approach

**Foundation:** Three Operating Systems (Physical, Rhythmic, Harmonic)
**Method:** Shapes → Names, Numbers → Letters, Tiny Loops
**Integration:** Every skill translates to production workflow
**Metrics:** Leading indicators (consistency, smoothness, comfort) > outcome goals

---

## Month 1: Foundation & Physical Setup
**Theme:** Establish physical foundation, bass clef basics, C major sandbox

### 1. Physical OS
- **Posture checklist:** bench height (elbows ~90°), seat distance, straight back
- **Hand shape drills:** curved fingers, thumb parallel to keys, relaxed wrist
- **Tension check:** 5-min daily scan (shoulders → forearms → fingers)

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **Metronome comfort:** Start 60 BPM, quarter notes
- **Internal pulse:** Tap foot while playing simple patterns
- **Subdivision intro:** Feel the "and" between beats (eighth notes)

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **C major scale:** Construction (W-W-H-W-W-W-H)
- **Diatonic triads in C:** I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii° (root position)
- **Chord spelling:** Name notes in C, F, G, Am triads

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **5-finger patterns:** C position (C-D-E-F-G), fluent ascending/descending
- **C major scale:** 1 octave, correct fingering (1-2-3-1-2-3-4-5)
- **Triad shapes:** C, F, G, Am (135 fingering)

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **5-finger patterns:** C position (C-B-A-G-F), mirror RH practice
- **Bass clef reading:** Landmarks (F, A, C, E, G in bass staff)
- **Root position bass notes:** Whole notes on C, F, G, A

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Static coordination:** RH plays C triad, LH plays C bass (whole note)
- **Simple shells:** RH melody (C-E-G), LH roots alternating
- **2-bar loops:** RH simple melody, LH quarter note roots

### 7. Reading (Treble Focus)
- **Treble clef:** All notes C4–C5 (middle C octave)
- **Bass clef:** Identify F3, A3, C4 (primary landmarks)
- **Read-ahead drill:** Eyes 1 note ahead of fingers

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Select Einaudi piece:** Choose one (e.g., "Nuvole Bianche," "Una Mattina")
- **First 4 bars:** Learn RH melody only, shape recognition
- **Slow tempo:** 40-50% of performance speed

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **Free play in C:** RH improvise over LH C drone
- **Emotional prompts:** Play "calm," "hopeful," "wistful" using white keys only
- **Shape exploration:** Move C triad shape up/down white keys

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Record MIDI:** Capture C major scale at different velocities → sample for tracks
- **Loop creation:** 4-bar I-IV-V-I progression → export to DAW
- **Velocity awareness:** Practice soft/medium/hard touch for dynamics

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Sessions per week:** Target 5/week
- **C major scale evenness:** Can play at 80 BPM, smooth
- **Triad comfort:** Name and play C, F, G, Am in <3 seconds each
- **Bass clef recall:** Identify 5 landmark notes without hesitation

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   Physical warm-up (posture, hand shape, scales)
5-12min:  Technique (RH/LH patterns, triad drills)
12-20min: Repertoire (4-bar chunk, slow reps)
20-27min: Harmony/reading (bass clef, triad spelling)
27-30min: Producer mode (quick improv/MIDI capture)
```

## Month 2: Bass Clef Fluency & Hand Integration
**Theme:** Strengthen bass clef reading, coordinate hands, expand harmonic vocab

### 1. Physical OS
- **Finger independence:** 5-4-3-2-1 descending patterns, each finger lifts cleanly
- **Thumb pivot:** Practice thumb-under for scale crossings (RH & LH)
- **Forearm rotation:** Natural motion for wider intervals

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **Eighth notes:** Play scales/triads in eighth note rhythm at 60 BPM
- **Simple syncopation:** Quarter-eighth-eighth patterns
- **Polyrhythm intro:** RH quarter notes, LH half notes (2:1)

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **Roman numeral fluency:** Recognize I-IV-V-I, I-vi-IV-V progressions by ear
- **A minor scale:** Natural, harmonic, melodic forms
- **Chord function:** Tonic (I, vi), Subdominant (IV, ii), Dominant (V, vii°)

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **C major scale:** 2 octaves, smooth thumb crossings
- **Inversions:** C, F, G triads (root, 1st, 2nd inversions)
- **Simple melodies:** 8-bar treble excerpts from Einaudi

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **C major scale:** 1 octave, correct fingering (5-4-3-2-1-3-2-1)
- **Bass clef fluency:** All notes F2–C4 (two octaves)
- **Chord shells:** LH plays root-5th (power chord shape)

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Shell voicings:** RH plays 3rd-7th, LH plays root (jazz style)
- **Alberti bass intro:** LH broken chord pattern while RH holds triad
- **8-bar loops:** RH melody + LH quarter note bass line

### 7. Reading
- **Bass clef:** Read simple bass lines (whole/half/quarter notes)
- **Grand staff:** Read both staves simultaneously (block chords only)
- **Lead sheet basics:** Chord symbols + melody notation

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Bars 1-8:** Hands separate, then together (very slow)
- **Harmonic analysis:** Identify chord progression (Roman numerals)
- **Micro-loop mastery:** 2-bar chunks at tempo, perfect 5x before moving on

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **I-IV-V-I loops:** Improvise RH melodies over repeating progression
- **A minor exploration:** Natural minor scale improvisation
- **Call & response:** 2-bar RH phrase, 2-bar LH response

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Chord voicing tricks:** Compare root position vs inversions for smoother progressions
- **DAW mapping:** Practice common prog house progressions (vi-IV-I-V)
- **Arpeggiation:** Play triads as 16th note arps → record MIDI

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Bass clef speed:** Read 4-bar bass line at sight (slow but accurate)
- **Hand coordination:** Play 4-bar piece hands-together without stopping
- **Inversion fluency:** Play C major triad in all 3 inversions in <5 seconds
- **Sessions:** Maintain 5/week consistency

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   Scales (C major 2 octaves RH, 1 octave LH)
5-10min:  Inversions drill (C, F, G through all positions)
10-22min: Repertoire (bars 1-8, slow integration)
22-27min: Bass clef reading exercises
27-30min: Improv over I-IV-V-I loop
```

## Month 3: Rhythm Mastery & G Major Expansion
**Theme:** Deepen rhythmic vocabulary, add G major, confident hands-together playing

### 1. Physical OS
- **Legato touch:** Connect notes smoothly, no gaps or overlaps
- **Staccato practice:** Crisp, short notes with relaxed wrist
- **Dynamic control:** pp to ff range on single notes and chords

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **Dotted rhythms:** Quarter-eighth patterns, eighth-quarter variations
- **Triplet feel:** Subdivide quarter notes into triplets at 70 BPM
- **Swing intro:** Feel the pocket (for Fred again.. style grooves)

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **G major scale & triads:** I, IV, V, vi in G (G, C, D, Em)
- **Borrowed chords:** Intro to modal interchange (Am in C major context)
- **7th chords:** Construction of maj7, min7, dom7 (theory only)

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **G major scale:** 2 octaves, smooth
- **Melodic patterns:** Sequences (1-2-3, 2-3-4, 3-4-5 etc.) in C & G
- **Octave jumps:** Clean leaps (C4 to C5) with relaxed arm

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **G major scale:** 1 octave
- **Walking bass lines:** Quarter note roots + passing tones (C-D-E-F-G)
- **Stride intro:** Root on 1 & 3, chord on 2 & 4 (simple)

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Contrary motion:** Hands move in opposite directions (scales, patterns)
- **Polyrhythm:** RH triplets, LH quarters (3:2 ratio)
- **Independent dynamics:** RH loud, LH soft (melody vs accompaniment)

### 7. Reading
- **Rhythmic reading:** Focus on dotted notes, ties, rests
- **Chord chart fluency:** See "C" → play triad instantly
- **Lead sheets:** Play 8-bar melody + chords from fake book style notation

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Bars 1-16:** Full integration at 60-70% tempo
- **Phrase shaping:** Identify natural breath points, musical sentences
- **Performance practice:** Play through without stopping (even with mistakes)

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **G major sandbox:** Improvise melodies over G-C-D-Em progression
- **Rhythmic variation:** Take 4-note motif, vary rhythm but keep pitches
- **Texture play:** Arpeggios, block chords, octaves on same progression

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Progressive house voicings:** Practice open 5ths, 9ths, sus chords
- **Sidechain simulation:** Play chords on off-beats (2 & 4)
- **Layer thinking:** Record bass line, then chords, then melody separately

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Repertoire fluency:** Bars 1-16 at 70% tempo, hands-together, musical
- **G major comfort:** Play scale and 4 triads without thinking
- **Rhythm accuracy:** Execute dotted and triplet patterns cleanly at 80 BPM
- **Reading speed:** Sight-read simple 8-bar pieces in C/G at 50% tempo

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   Rhythm drills (dotted patterns, triplets with metronome)
5-10min:  G major work (scale, triads, progressions)
10-22min: Repertoire (bars 1-16, musical practice)
22-27min: Lead sheet reading (new simple tunes)
27-30min: Producer mode (progressive house chord exploration)
```

## Month 4: Velocity Control & D Major
**Theme:** Expressive dynamics, add D major, introduce 7th chords physically

### 1. Physical OS
- **Weight transfer:** Play forte with arm weight, not finger force
- **Touch sensitivity:** Practice pp-mf-ff on single keys
- **Velocity consistency:** Play scales where every note is exact same volume

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **16th notes:** Simple patterns at 60 BPM
- **Syncopation:** Emphasize off-beats in melodies and chords
- **Groove pockets:** Feel the "laid-back" vs "pushing" timing

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **D major scale & triads:** I, IV, V, vi in D (D, G, A, Bm)
- **7th chords:** Play maj7, min7, dom7 in C major (Cmaj7, Dm7, G7, Am7)
- **Functional progressions:** I-vi-ii-V, I-V-vi-IV in multiple keys

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **D major scale:** 2 octaves
- **7th chord voicings:** Root position (1-3-5-7 fingering)
- **Expressive melody:** Play Einaudi RH with dynamic shaping

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **D major scale:** 1 octave
- **7th chord roots + 7th:** LH plays root-7th shells
- **Bass patterns:** Quarter-eighth combos, walking lines in D

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **RH melody + LH 7th shells:** Practice I-vi-ii-V with shells
- **Dynamic balance:** LH softer than RH in lyrical passages
- **Rubato intro:** Subtle tempo flexibility (not with metronome)

### 7. Reading
- **Sharp keys:** Fluent in G major (1#), D major (2#) notation
- **7th chord symbols:** Read Cmaj7, Dm7, G7 from lead sheets
- **Articulation marks:** Slurs, accents, tenuto in notation

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Full piece:** Play through entire Einaudi piece at 50-60% tempo
- **Dynamic mapping:** Mark pp, mp, mf, f throughout score
- **Section work:** Perfect the most challenging 4-bar section

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **7th chord colors:** Solo over Cmaj7-Am7-Dm7-G7 vamp
- **Dynamic improv:** Play same phrase pp, then ff, notice feeling difference
- **Modal playing:** Try Dorian mode (D-E-F-G-A-B-C-D) for Bicep-style grooves

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Velocity layer creation:** Record same melody at different velocities
- **7th chord lushness:** Use maj7/min7 for atmospheric pads
- **MIDI velocity editing:** Compare played vs programmed feel

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Full piece run-through:** Complete Einaudi piece start-to-finish at 60% tempo
- **Dynamic range:** Demonstrate clean pp to ff on demand
- **7th chord fluency:** Play Cmaj7, Dm7, Em7, Fmaj7, G7, Am7 in <10 seconds
- **3-key comfort:** Fluent scales/triads in C, G, D major

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   Dynamic drills (scales pp to ff)
5-10min:  7th chord voicings (all positions in C major)
10-23min: Repertoire (full piece, slow + expressive)
23-28min: D major work (scale, progressions)
28-30min: Improv with 7th chords
```

## Month 5: Minor Keys Deep Dive & Pedal Technique
**Theme:** Master A and E minor, introduce sustain pedal, emotional expression

### 1. Physical OS
- **Pedal mechanics:** Heel down, ball of foot on pedal, half-pedaling
- **Pedal timing:** Press after note, release before chord change
- **Sitting stability:** Maintain posture while using pedal

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **Complex syncopation:** Bicep/Fred again.. style off-beat patterns
- **Tempo modulation:** Practice speeding up/slowing down gradually
- **Metric modulation intro:** Feel 3/4 vs 4/4 vs 6/8

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **A minor:** Natural, harmonic, melodic scales + triads
- **E minor:** All forms + diatonic triads
- **Minor progressions:** i-iv-V, i-VI-III-VII (emotional EDM progressions)

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **Minor scale mastery:** A & E natural/harmonic/melodic, 2 octaves
- **Minor 7th arpeggios:** Am7, Em7 (4-note arpeggios)
- **Expressive ornaments:** Grace notes, simple trills

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **Minor scales:** A & E minor, 1 octave each form
- **Pedal point bass:** Hold low A or E while RH moves through chords
- **Minor chord voicings:** Root position & inversions

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Pedal integration:** Simple pieces with pedal (change on chord shifts)
- **Melody + arpeggiated accompaniment:** RH melody, LH broken chords
- **Emotional phrasing:** Play phrases that "breathe" (lift, land)

### 7. Reading
- **Minor key signatures:** Read pieces in Am, Em fluently
- **Accidentals:** Sharp/flat/natural symbols in harmonic/melodic minor
- **Pedal notation:** Understand Ped., *, Ped.-simile markings

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Target tempo:** 70-80% of performance speed
- **Pedaling:** Add pedal to Einaudi piece (or similar atmospheric piece)
- **Emotional intention:** Play with feeling, not just notes

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **Minor mood improv:** Sad, dark, contemplative over Am vamp
- **Bicep-style loops:** i-VI-III-VII in Am (Am-F-C-G)
- **Pedal wash:** Use pedal to create atmospheric textures

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Minor key production:** Understand progressive house's love of minor
- **Emotional arcs:** Build tension/release through progressions
- **Reverb simulation:** Heavy pedal use = natural reverb

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Minor fluency:** A & E minor scales (all forms) at 90 BPM
- **Pedal control:** Clean chord changes with pedal, no mud
- **Repertoire speed:** 80% tempo, with pedal, musically
- **Minor progressions:** Play i-iv-V, i-VI-III-VII in Am/Em instantly

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   Minor scales without pedal
5-10min:  Pedal technique drills (simple chord progressions)
10-23min: Repertoire with pedal integration
23-28min: Minor key harmony exploration
28-30min: Dark/emotional improv in Am
```

## Month 6: Milestone Review & Performance Readiness
**Theme:** Consolidate 5 months, polish repertoire piece, first "performance"

### 1. Physical OS
- **Tension audit:** Video yourself, identify any shoulder/wrist tension
- **Endurance:** Can play 30 minutes without fatigue
- **Efficiency:** Minimal motion, maximum sound

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **All learned rhythms:** Quarters, eighths, 16ths, dotted, triplets at 90 BPM
- **Musical timing:** Can play slightly ahead/behind beat intentionally
- **Time feel:** Develop personal sense of groove

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **5-key mastery:** C, G, D, A minor, E minor (scales, triads, 7ths)
- **Progression library:** 10 common progressions memorized in multiple keys
- **Functional hearing:** Recognize I-IV-V-vi by ear in songs

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **Technical review:** All scales 2 octaves at 100 BPM, clean
- **7th arpeggios:** All major & minor 7ths in 5 keys
- **Melodic fluency:** Play Einaudi RH from memory

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **Bass clef mastery:** Read bass clef as easily as treble
- **All scale forms:** LH scales in 5 keys at 90 BPM
- **Accompaniment patterns:** Alberti, stride, block, broken chords

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Repertoire from memory:** Einaudi piece memorized, hands-together
- **Polyrhythmic control:** 3:2, 4:3 ratios fluent
- **Independent articulation:** RH legato, LH staccato (and vice versa)

### 7. Reading
- **Sight-reading:** Simple pieces in 5 keys at 60% tempo
- **Lead sheets:** Play jazz/pop standards from chord charts
- **No-hesitation reading:** Treble + bass staff simultaneously

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Performance tempo:** 90-100% of intended speed
- **Memory security:** Can start from any section
- **Musical maturity:** Dynamic shaping, phrasing, emotion present

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **Free form:** 5-minute improvisation without plan (record it)
- **Style-specific:** Einaudi-style minimalist piece creation
- **Producer sketches:** 3 original chord progressions for future tracks

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Full loop creation:** Compose complete 16-bar loop (intro-verse structure)
- **MIDI performance:** Record full progression with human feel
- **Sound design thinking:** Which piano tones = what synth patches?

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Repertoire readiness:** Can perform Einaudi piece for others confidently
- **5-key fluency:** Instant recall of scales/chords in C, G, D, Am, Em
- **Reading speed:** Sight-read Grade 1-2 pieces
- **Session consistency:** 5/week maintained for 6 months

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session) + MILESTONE
```
Regular session:
0-5min:   Technical review (scales, arpeggios)
5-25min:  Repertoire performance practice (run-throughs)
25-30min: Creative improv

MILESTONE EVENT:
- Record video of full Einaudi piece
- Share with friend/family (or just for yourself)
- Reflect on 6-month progress
- Set intentions for months 7-12
```

## Month 7: Left-Hand Independence & F Major
**Theme:** Elevate LH to equal partner, add F major, counterpoint intro

### 1. Physical OS
- **LH strength:** Finger exercises specifically for weaker LH fingers (4, 5)
- **Balance:** Equal tone quality between hands
- **Crossed-hand technique:** RH over LH and vice versa

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **Rhythmic counterpoint:** Different rhythms in each hand simultaneously
- **Hemiola:** 3 against 2 patterns (waltz vs march feel)
- **Swing refinement:** Shuffle feels for blues/jazz influenced playing

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **F major scale & triads:** I, IV, V, vi in F (F, Bb, C, Dm)
- **Secondary dominants:** V/V, V/vi concepts (theory + sound)
- **Chord substitutions:** Replace IV with ii, etc.

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **F major scale:** 2 octaves (B♭ awareness)
- **Melodic independence:** RH carries tune while LH has counter-melody
- **Extended arpeggios:** 2-octave arpeggios in F major chords

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **F major scale:** 2 octaves (build strength)
- **LH melody practice:** Play simple melodies in bass clef (typically RH role)
- **Complex bass patterns:** Syncopated, 16th-note bass lines

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Two-voice invention style:** Each hand has independent melodic line
- **Call & response:** RH phrase, LH answers, then reverse
- **Polymetric:** RH in 4/4, LH feels 3/4 (or similar)

### 7. Reading
- **Bass clef speed:** Read LH melodies as fast as treble
- **Two-voice music:** Read simple Bach-style two-part inventions
- **Flat key signatures:** F major (1♭) and B♭ major (2♭)

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Einaudi mastery:** Polish to performance-ready, record final version
- **Second piece:** Start learning new piece with prominent LH (e.g., Einaudi "I Giorni")
- **LH feature:** Practice sections where LH has melody

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **LH lead improv:** LH plays melody, RH plays chords (role reversal)
- **F major exploration:** Create progressions in F
- **Counterpoint play:** Improvise two independent lines

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Bass line composition:** Write compelling bass lines for tracks
- **Counter-melodies:** Create answering phrases for existing toplines
- **F major in house music:** Explore flat key warmth in productions

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **LH independence:** LH can play melodies smoothly, not just bass/chords
- **F major fluency:** Scales, triads, progressions instant recall
- **Two-voice reading:** Can read simple two-part music slowly
- **Repertoire milestone:** Einaudi piece #1 fully polished

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   LH-focused warm-up (scales, finger strength)
5-10min:  F major work (scales, progressions)
10-20min: New repertoire piece (LH focus)
20-27min: Two-voice reading exercises
27-30min: LH melody improvisation
```

## Month 8: Extended Chords & B♭ Major
**Theme:** 9ths, 11ths, 13ths for lush production, add B♭ major, voicing sophistication

### 1. Physical OS
- **Wider stretches:** Practice 9th and 10th intervals (slowly)
- **Chord voicing shifts:** Move between extended chord shapes smoothly
- **Arm rotation:** For large chord jumps

### 2. Rhythmic OS
- **Tuplet variations:** Quintuplets, sextuplets (expressive timing)
- **Metric modulation:** Smooth transitions between tempos
- **Rhythmic displacement:** Play phrase starting on different beats

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **B♭ major scale & triads:** I, IV, V, vi in B♭
- **Extended chords:** 9th, 11th, 13th construction and sound
- **Voicing techniques:** Drop-2, drop-3, rootless voicings

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **B♭ major scale:** 2 octaves
- **Extended chord shapes:** maj9, min9, dom9, dom13 voicings
- **Rootless voicings:** RH plays 3-5-7-9 while LH plays root

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **B♭ major scale:** 2 octaves
- **Extended bass notes:** LH plays root + extensions (9th, 13th)
- **Walking bass:** Jazz-style walking in B♭

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Shell + extensions:** LH root-7th, RH 3rd-5th-9th
- **Lush progressions:** Play extended chord progressions (I△9-vi9-ii9-V13)
- **Spacing awareness:** Avoid muddy low-register extensions

### 7. Reading
- **Extended chord symbols:** Read C△9, Dm11, G13 from charts
- **B♭ major reading:** Sight-read in 2-flat key signature
- **Jazz charts:** Navigate lead sheets with extensions

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Second piece progress:** Bars 1-16 of new LH-prominent piece
- **Add extensions:** Reimagine Einaudi piece with subtle 9ths
- **Musical depth:** Focus on color and atmosphere

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **Extended chord improv:** Solo over ii9-V13-I△9 progressions
- **B♭ major sketches:** Write 8-bar progression ideas
- **Atmospheric pads:** Use sustained extended chords with pedal

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Lush chord production:** Extended chords for progressive house breakdowns
- **Voicing for mixing:** Understand which extensions work in dense mixes
- **MIDI chord library:** Build collection of extended chord MIDI files

### 11. Progress Metrics
- **Extended chord fluency:** Play maj9, min9, dom9, dom13 in multiple keys
- **B♭ major comfort:** Scales and progressions without hesitation
- **Voicing sophistication:** Can create 3-4 different voicings of same chord
- **6-key mastery:** C, G, D, F, B♭ major + Am, Em minor

### 12. Practice Routine (30-min session)
```
0-5min:   Extended chord drills (all inversions)
5-10min:  B♭ major scale & progressions
10-22min: Repertoire (second piece, chord embellishment)
22-28min: Voicing exercises (same progression, different voicings)
28-30min: Atmospheric improv with extensions
```

## Month 10: Jazz Harmony & Improvisation Depth
**Theme:** ii-V-I mastery, jazz voicings, spontaneous melodic creation

### 1. Physical OS
- **Jazz hand positions:** Wider spacing, thumb awareness for 10ths
- **Quick voicing changes:** Smooth transitions in fast chord progressions
- **Comping technique:** Rhythmic chord stabs (RH), bass (LH)

### 2. Rhythmic OS

- **Anticipations:** Play chords just before the beat (1/16th early)

### 3. Harmonic OS
- **ii-V-I in all keys:** Minor and major versions
- **Tritone substitution:** Replace V with ♭II7

### 4. Right Hand Work
- **Jazz licks:** Learn 5 classic ii-V-I melodic phrases

- **Rootless voicings:** 3-7-9, 7-9-13 formations

### 5. Left Hand Work
- **Shell voicings:** Root-7th, root-3rd essential jazz voicings
- **Walking bass:** Create walking bass lines in 10 standards
- **Stride:** Full stride pattern at 120 BPM

### 6. Hand Coordination
- **Comping:** LH bass, RH chords on off-beats
- **Solo piano:** RH improvises, LH walks and comps
- **Shell + melody:** LH shells, RH melodic improvisation

### 7. Reading
- **Jazz charts:** Read Real Book style lead sheets

### 8. Repertoire Piece
- **Jazz standard:** Learn 1 simple jazz standard (e.g., "Autumn Leaves")

- **Lead sheet performance:** Play melody + chord changes

### 9. Improvisation/Creativity
- **ii-V-I improvisation:** Solo over changes for 10 minutes
- **Blues improvisation:** 12-bar blues in multiple keys
- **Free jazz exploration:** Atonal, experimental playing

### 10. Producer Integration
- **Jazz in electronic music:** Fred again.. jazz samples, soulful chords
- **Reharmonization:** Take simple progression, jazz it up
- **Sampling ideas:** Record jazz licks for chopping/sampling

### 11. Progress Metrics

- **Walking bass:** Create musical walking line on standards
- **Improvisation length:** Solo over changes for 5+ min without repeating
- **Jazz standard:** Play 1 standard with melody + changes

## Appendix: Practice Philosophy Reminders

**Always Return To:**
- Three Operating Systems (Physical, Rhythmic, Harmonic)
- Shapes before names
- Numbers/functions over letters
- Tiny loops, perfect reps
- Slow + correct > fast + sloppy
- Constrained sandbox (depth > breadth)
- Interleaved practice (variety in session)
- Self-recording for feedback
- Producer mode mapping (piano → production)
- Leading indicators (consistency, smoothness, comfort)

**Success Defined:**
After 12 months, you can:
1. Play piano with clean technique and musicality
2. Read music in multiple keys confidently
3. Understand functional harmony and apply it
4. Perform 5+ pieces from memory with expression
5. Improvise coherently in multiple styles
6. Use piano as primary compositional tool for production
7. Practice sustainably and joyfully for years to come

**This is not the end. This is the foundation for a lifetime of musical growth.**
